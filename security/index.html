Security

Report a vulnerability.

Found a security issue in IncidentGuard? Tell us privately — we fix first, then credit you.

IncidentGuard is a hackathon prototype built at the TUM Blockchain & AI Hackathon 2026. Items in [BRACKETS] are placeholders to be completed before any real launch.

How to report

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on our repository. Your report is visible only to the maintainers.

Report privately on GitHub

Please don't open a public issue for security problems.

What to include

  • What you found and where (page, endpoint or component).
  • Steps to reproduce.
  • What an attacker could do with it.
  • Your name or handle, if you'd like credit.

In scope

  • This website, the portal and the IncidentGuard API.
  • Payment verification — e.g. reusing a payment, paying less than quoted, or getting a report without paying.
  • Access control — e.g. an agent seeing evidence it was not granted.

Out of scope

  • Denial-of-service and load testing.
  • Social engineering of the team.
  • The BSV network itself and third-party wallets.

Our promise

If you act in good faith and follow this page, we won't pursue legal action. We'll confirm we received your report, keep you updated, and credit you once it's fixed if you want us to.
