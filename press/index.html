Short description

IncidentGuard is a pay-per-investigation security service. Users and AI agents ask a security question, see a fixed price, approve one payment in their BSV wallet and receive a report with findings, sources and stated limitations. Each payment is bound to exactly one request and cannot be reused. A non-custodial Growth Pool lets liquidity providers fund capped investigation credits for approved agents. IncidentGuard was built by pavLex at the TUM Blockchain & AI Hackathon 2026 in Munich.
