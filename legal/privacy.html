Privacy.

What this prototype collects, and what it does not.

IncidentGuard is a hackathon prototype built at the TUM Blockchain & AI Hackathon 2026. Items in [BRACKETS] are placeholders to be completed before any real launch.

The short version

This site does not use accounts, analytics, tracking or advertising. The portal is a demo: the question you type stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere.

What is processed

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when serving pages. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts. Pages load the Inter and JetBrains Mono fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers and shares your IP address with them.
  • Portal demo. Questions, wallet steps and reports in the demo run entirely in your browser. Nothing is stored or transmitted.

When the real service launches

Investigation requests, payment transactions and audit logs will be processed to deliver reports. Payments are public on the BSV blockchain by nature. This page will be updated before any real data is processed.
